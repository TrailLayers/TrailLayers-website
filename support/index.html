Frequently asked questions

How do I add a garment to my wardrobe?

Tap the + button on the Wardrobe tab. You can add a photo from your camera roll or take one in-app, then fill in details like category, insulation, waterproofing, and size. Fields you don't know can be left blank and edited later.

How do I build an outfit?

Go to the Outfits tab and tap +. Give the outfit a name, then select garments from your wardrobe to include. Saved outfits can be reused across activity logs so you don't have to re-select the same gear every time.

How do I log an activity?

Tap + on the Activity Logs tab. Select the garments or an existing outfit you wore, then add weather conditions, effort level, and comfort feedback. Ratings you enter are used to surface garment insights over time.

Can I share an outfit publicly?

Yes. Each outfit has a privacy toggle in its detail view. When set to public, a shareable link is generated that anyone can view without needing the app. You can switch it back to private at any time.

Is my gear data private by default?

Yes. Your wardrobe, activity logs, and outfits are private to your account. Only outfits you explicitly mark as public are visible to others. See our Privacy Policy for the full details on data storage.

What iOS version does TrailLayers require?

TrailLayers requires iOS 17 or later and is optimized for iPhone. iPad is not currently supported.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your profile, wardrobe, outfits, and activity logs. The action cannot be undone.

I found a bug or something isn't working — what should I do?

Use the contact form below and describe what happened, what you expected, and your iOS version. Screenshots are helpful if you can attach them. We'll follow up by email.
